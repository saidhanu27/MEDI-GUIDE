About MediGuide AI

MediGuide AI is an AI-powered symptom analysis system developed for educational purposes. It provides possible health conditions, general medicine information, food suggestions, and guidance on the type of doctor to consult. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice.

The core diagnostic system is powered by an Intelligent Clinical AI Engine, trained on comprehensive clinical symptom-to-condition healthcare data.
