Clinical Decision Support
📌 1. Problem Statement
❌
Access Barrier: Patients often delay doctor visits due to lack of immediate triage, long queues, and rural accessibility gaps.
❌
Language & Complexity: Most health checkers are in English only, text-heavy, and fail without active internet.
✅
Our Solution: MediGuide AI gives instant multilingual voice triage, emergency risk assessment, certified PDF reports, and digital OPD token booking.
⚙️ 2. Working (Step-by-Step)
  • Patient Input: Enter age/gender and speak or select symptoms in Telugu, Hindi, or English.
  • Clinical Triage: Evaluates multi-symptom combinations against clinical hallmark criteria.
  • Risk & Health Score: Calculates Low/Med/High risk and an AI Wellness Index (0-100 gauge).
  • Actionable Care: Listen to voice summary, download official PDF report, and book OPD token.
